01 The Core Asymmetry

Checking is cheap.
Finding is the wall.

NP is the class of problems whose yes-answers come with short certificates. Below is a graph hiding a Hamiltonian cycle — a loop visiting every node exactly once. Handing you the cycle makes verification a walk in the park. Finding it from scratch means searching a space of n! candidates. That gap is the entire question.

What to feel: verification lights the certificate cycle in a handful of steps — always. The solver backtracks through partial paths; watch search steps climb against a space that grows factorially as you drag n upward. Real SAT solvers are far smarter than this brute force — but the worst case is exponential, and fifty years of effort says nobody has beaten that for NP-complete problems.
